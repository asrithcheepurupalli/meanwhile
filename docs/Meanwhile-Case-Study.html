A MADE. STUDIO PORTFOLIO PIECE01 — MEANWHILE
THE CONCEPTA NEW CATEGORY

Leasing offered two answers. We added a third.

A unit is either occupied or vacant — earning full rent, or nothing at all. Meanwhile introduces the in-between: temporary occupancy that pays the owner from day one and keeps every option open.

FOR THE OWNER

Earn during the vacancy

List a vacant unit at a reduced "meanwhile" rate and earn income while you keep hunting for the full-rent tenant — with total flexibility and a clear notice period written into the contract.

FOR THE BUSINESS

A premium address, half the rent

Move into a high-footfall location you couldn't otherwise afford. Test the market on a short commitment — then vacate on notice, or convert to the full lease when you're ready.
